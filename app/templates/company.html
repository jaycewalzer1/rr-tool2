Why We Built CRE: AI Solutions

As a lean real estate operating company based in Charlotte with approximately $1B in AUM, our team needed a comprehensive, all-in-one software solution to centralize market data, manage our pipeline, and generate internal reports. We believe our experience as operators gives us a unique advantage in tailoring the platform to be highly user-friendly—featuring AI-driven data extraction, document parsing, intuitive dashboards, and smart tools that know what to extract and where to find it.

Our Foundation

Alignment of Expertise

One co-founder is a principal and founder of Collett Capital, a real estate investment, development, and operations firm focused on build-to-rent, multifamily, and commercial properties across the Southeast and Southwest. Having deployed over $1B in rental housing and commercial real estate, he brings a deep understanding of valuation, due diligence, and the key data points that drive investment certainty. The other co-founder has a background in computer science, with experience at alternative data companies, private credit firms, and a national asset manager. His unique skill set bridges the gap between data engineering and investment execution, with a focus on identifying valuable data and building systems for efficient extraction, storage, and rapid recall of critical information.

Mission-Driven

We exist to empower real estate companies by helping them organize, track, and leverage their internal data—often overlooked or underutilized—to make smarter, faster investment decisions. Our goal is to level the playing field, shifting away from a “pay-to-win” model toward one where firms can gather, store, and activate the data they already own. By eliminating the need for manual tracking and input, we help unlock insights that drive better outcomes with less effort.

Focused on Results

Whether it’s storing offering memorandums to extract commercial lease terms and durations, or uploading broker industry reports to capture submarket vacancy data, our platform enables data extraction across all document types. The system intelligently interprets uploaded files and pulls the relevant information—streamlining access to the insights that matter.
